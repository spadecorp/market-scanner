SPaDe Corp | Market Scanner 1.0.3
Windows 10/11, 64-bit Intel or AMD

Run the Windows setup EXE to install Market Scanner for your account.
If you downloaded the portable ZIP instead, follow these steps.

1. Choose Extract All on the downloaded ZIP.
2. Open the extracted Market-Scanner folder.
3. Double-click Market Scanner.exe. Keep the other files beside it.

Connect to the internet for the first refresh. The window opens while it fetches
the data. Sector ETFs and S&P 500 stocks are checked first. The full US listing
scan can take hours, depending on the connection and Yahoo's request limits.
The session date is the last completed US session reported by the SPY feed.
The app waits 15 minutes after the reported regular-session close before using
the current day's bar. The progress bar shows the percentage of listed symbols with required data
saved for this session. It remains partially filled when downloads fail.
A partial scan can gain or lose top-300 entries as the rest of the market loads.

Data is cached under %APPDATA%\SPaDe Market Scanner. Reopening the app
displays the saved session and starts a refresh. Refresh data checks for a new
session or retries the symbols missing from the current session. If Yahoo limits
requests, the app keeps its progress and retries automatically while open.

Volume Scanner: daily close >= $1 and volume >= 1,000,000. A tie counts as a
record. History high compares with available data since January 2010; 52-week
and quarter compare with the preceding 365 and 91 calendar days. The current
bar is excluded from the baselines. Buy % locates the close in the day's range.
RS is the weighted cross-sectional relative strength percentile among loaded
symbols with enough history. Short float is optional provider metadata.
Use the column controls, filters, sorting or CSV export to inspect results.

Open Sector Heatmap inside Volume Scanner to group the filtered results.
Tile intensity measures qualifying ticker count. Pick a tile
to see that sector's scanner results. Unclassified means sector metadata is
unavailable. The ETF group holds funds without an equity-sector classification.

Sector Performance: 30 sector and thematic ETFs, with 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M and YTD
adjusted returns. Select a broad sector to open its scanner results.
The S&P 500 stage table and trend use price versus the 150-day average and the
slope of that average. Select any populated stage count for stock details.

Market Monitor: 30 sessions of breadth, with counts for daily, monthly,
34-session and quarterly moves, 5-/10-day ratios, 10x prior ATR, the share
above the 50-day average, observed constituent count and SPY close. Use a
nonzero count to inspect, search, group or export the contributing stocks.
Green and red shading follow the bullish and bearish breadth bands. Purple
marks ATR extensions. Historical breadth uses the constituent list loaded for the scan.

Quotes are downloaded directly to the user's computer for personal research.
Providers may delay data, omit symbols, or limit requests. This build is
unsigned. Windows or organizational policy may display a publisher warning.
